## Keep the backlog current

`docs/BACKLOG.md` is the living to-do list. With every change you commit:

- move the item between **Up next**, **In progress** and **Done** (with the date and branch or PR),
- add new work you discover to **Up next** or **Later**,
- add one line to the **Change log** (newest first),
- update the "Last updated" date.

Update `docs/PROGRESS.md` too when a change is significant (a new feature, a design or cost decision, new measured results).
